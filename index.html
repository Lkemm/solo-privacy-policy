Privacy Policy

1. Information we collect

solo is designed to store business records locally on your device. We do not collect or sell your personal or business data. Your data remains under your control on your device.DEVICE PERMISSIONS .

2. Local storage

Financial data, quotes, invoices, expenses, and other records may be stored locally in your app storage. This data is not transmitted to us for advertising or analytics purposes.- Camera / Storage: Used exclusively for scanning receipts locally and attaching receipt images to your expense records. Receipts remain on your device unless you choose to export or backup to your personal Google Drive. Notifications: Used strictly for local, on-device overdue payment alarms and follow-up reminders scheduled by you

3. Third parties

We do not share, sell, or rent personal or business data to third parties. No advertising network or data broker is used by this app.

4. Data deletion

You may delete your local records from within the app or by clearing app/browser storage. If you uninstall the app, local data may be removed with it.
